Who we are

Business support meets technical delivery.

NM OnePoint Services covers five service categories. Business & tax services include GST registration and returns, Udyam, LUT, Professional Tax, PAN, TAN, IEC and digital signatures. Engineering & BIM services include AutoCAD 2D drafting, Scan to BIM, Point Cloud to BIM, Revit modeling, clash detection and quantity take-off.

Online government & digital services, computer repair & IT support, and GIS services with QGIS and ArcGIS complete the offering. Each engagement is scoped and agreed before work begins, with documented requirements and regular updates until handover.
