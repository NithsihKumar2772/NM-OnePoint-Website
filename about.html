About NM OnePoint

A single point for business and technical work

NM OnePoint Services brings routine business support together with technical services, so clients coordinate compliance, IT, BIM, and GIS needs through one point of contact.

Who we are

Business support meets technical delivery

NM OnePoint Services covers two sides of everyday project and business needs. On the business side: GST, Udyam, Professional Tax, and general business support. On the technical side: BIM services including Scan to BIM and Revit, and GIS solutions including QGIS and ArcGIS work.

Each engagement is scoped and agreed before work begins, with documented requirements and regular updates until handover.
